DEALERS 7 reasons
D Defective signal Signal cannot be cleared by normal means
E Electric train Proceed towards an obstruction or isolated area

L Level crossing Signal at or protecting a level crossing
E Examine the line Sent forward to examine the line ahead
R Restrictive acceptance Absolute block only — signaller gives authority, line may be occupied
S Shunting Movement for shunting purposes only
